Terms and policies.

Last updated 2026

Licence

When you buy a pack you get a personal, non-transferable licence to use the presets on your own photographs and on photographs you edit for clients.

  • Allowed: personal work, client work, social media, prints and publications you create.
  • Not allowed: sharing, reselling, giving away or repackaging the preset files, alone or bundled with other products.

The sample photographs on this site and inside the packs remain the property of Matan Wild Photography and may not be used without written permission.

Refunds

Presets are digital files delivered instantly. If a pack doesn't work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase. We'll help you get it working, and if we can't, we'll refund you. Refunded orders lose access to the files.

Terms of sale

Orders are processed by our online reseller, Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record and handles payment, invoicing and sales tax. Prices are shown in the currency listed on each product page. You'll receive a receipt by email after every purchase.

Packs you buy include free updates to that pack. We may add, change or retire packs at any time; anything you've already bought stays in your library.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to sell and deliver your presets: your name, email, country and what you bought. Payment details are handled by the checkout provider and never reach us.

We use your email to deliver your files, answer support requests and, if you agreed, send occasional field notes and new releases. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Our service providers: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Lemon Squeezy (checkout), MailerLite (email), Make.com and monday.com (order handling). To see, correct or delete your data, write to support.

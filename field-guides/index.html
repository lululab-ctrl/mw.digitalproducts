What's in the set

Four guides. Four skills every wildlife photographer needs.

01

Composition

Where to place it, what to leave out and how to lead the eye. 39 lessons.

02

Colour

Harmony, temperature, saturation and white balance. 36 lessons and 10 theory pages.

03

Light

Direction, quality, time of day and exposure. 25 lessons and 8 light tools.

04

Planning

Seasons, sky, apps, fieldcraft and gear. 37 lessons and 13 planning tools.

Why it works

A whole lesson on one page.

On every page: a real photo, a drawing that shows what is happening in it, and a line or two you can remember in the field. Every lesson ends with "Break the rule": when to go against it, and how.

One idea Drawn on the photo A real photo

On every screen

On your desk, your tablet and your phone in the field.

PDFs that open on any device. Study on a big screen at home, then open the cheat sheet on your phone just before sunrise.

Who teaches you

A wildlife photographer who lives in the field.

Matan Wild is an adventurer, a nature and wildlife photographer and a photo tour leader in Israel and around the world, and the founder of MW Photo Tours. Every photo in the guides is his, and every idea was tested on real animals, in real light, in the field.

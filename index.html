04Questions

Good to know.

Which programs do the presets work in?
Each pack lists its compatibility on its page. Packs include .xmp files for Lightroom Classic, Lightroom and Photoshop's Camera Raw filter; packs that also include mobile or LUT versions say so.
How do I get my files after paying?
You get an email straight away. Open My downloads, enter the email you paid with, and click the sign-in link we send. Your packs are there to download.
Can I download again later?
Yes. Sign in to My downloads any time to download again, including future updates to packs you own.
Will it look exactly like the samples?
Every camera and scene is different. Presets get you most of the way in one click; small exposure and white balance tweaks finish the job.
What if it doesn't work for me?
Write to support and we'll help. Our refund policy has the details.

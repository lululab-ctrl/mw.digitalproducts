Questions

Which programs do the products work with?
Every product page lists what it works with under "Works with". Presets work in Lightroom Classic, Lightroom and Photoshop's Camera Raw; the plugin and the guides state their own requirements.
How do I get my files after paying?
You get an email straight away. Open My downloads, enter the email you paid with, and click the sign-in link we send. Everything you bought is there.
Can I download again later?
Yes. Sign in to My downloads any time, including for future updates to products you own.
Do I need an account?
No password to create. Your purchases are tied to your email, and you sign in with a one-time link.
What if something doesn't work for me?
Write to support and we'll help. Our refund policy has the details.
